Admissions

Join War Wounded Academy and begin a journey of learning, discipline, and excellence.

Admissions Are Open

War Wounded Academy Grafton SSS welcomes applications from pupils and students seeking quality education in a disciplined and supportive environment.

Primary

Admission is open for learners ready to build strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, discipline, and creativity.

Junior Secondary

Admission is open for students transitioning into structured secondary learning and academic development.

Senior Secondary

Admission is open for students preparing for advanced learning, examinations, leadership, and future opportunities.

How to Apply

Follow these simple steps to begin the admission process.

01

Contact the School

Reach out to the school office to request admission information and available spaces.

02

Submit Requirements

Provide the required documents and basic learner information for review.

03

Assessment / Review

The school may review the learner’s level or conduct a simple assessment where necessary.

04

Complete Registration

Once accepted, complete registration and prepare for the start of learning.

Admission Requirements

What Parents / Guardians May Need

Admission requirements may vary depending on the level of entry. Parents and guardians are encouraged to contact the school for updated details.

  • Completed admission inquiry or application information
  • Previous school record or report card where available
  • Birth certificate or identification document where available
  • Parent / guardian contact details
  • Any additional document requested by the school administration

Ready to Start the Admission Process?

Contact the school today and learn more about available spaces and requirements.
